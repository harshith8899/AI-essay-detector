My grandmother kept missing her medication times, so I built her a clunky little app over one winter break that just buzzed her phone at the right hours. My first version crashed constantly and sent notifications at 3 a.m. twice before I fixed the bug. She still calls it 'the pill thing' and shows it off to her neighbors like it's magic, even though it's maybe two hundred lines of code held together with patience. It's not sophisticated. But it's the first thing I ever built that another person actually depends on.
