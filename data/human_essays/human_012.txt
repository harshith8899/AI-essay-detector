The lot next to our apartment building was just weeds and broken glass until three of us neighbors got tired of looking at it. We had no idea what we were doing and killed most of our first tomato plants by overwatering them out of pure anxiety. Mrs. Okafor from the third floor turned out to know everything about soil, and she basically became our unofficial teacher. Two summers later the garden feeds six families and somehow also became the place where people actually talk to their neighbors, which the lot never did as weeds.
