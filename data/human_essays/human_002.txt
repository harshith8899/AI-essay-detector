My first debate round lasted four minutes before I forgot my entire argument and just stood there. The judge actually put her pen down. I wanted to quit that night, but my partner texted me a single word: 'again.' So we drilled rebuttals in her basement every Tuesday for a month. I lost my next six rounds too, honestly, but by the regional tournament I could feel the argument forming while the other team was still talking. We didn't win state. We got to the semifinal, and I didn't freeze once, which felt like winning anyway.
