Our robot's arm snapped off thirty seconds into the qualifying round, in front of the one judge who'd been the toughest on us all season. My teammate wanted to cry, and honestly so did I. We spent that night in a hotel bathroom with a hot glue gun and a roll of duct tape trying anything. It held for exactly one more match, which was enough to not finish last. We didn't advance. But I learned more about torque, and about staying calm when something breaks in front of everyone, than any match we actually won.
