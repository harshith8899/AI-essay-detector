The first dog I worked with at the shelter bit through my glove, and I almost didn't come back the next Saturday. But nobody there acted like that was a big deal, so I showed up anyway. Six months later that same dog, Biscuit, fell asleep with his head on my shoe during a slow shift. I've cleaned more kennels than I can count and cried in the parking lot twice when dogs got adopted, which doesn't make sense but happens every time. I'm not sure I'm helping the shelter as much as it's helping me.
