I moved here when I was eleven and understood maybe one word out of ten in my first English class. I used to write down phrases phonetically in the back of my notebook just to survive lunch conversations. My teacher, Ms. Reyes, let me answer questions in writing before I could say them out loud, which sounds small but wasn't. By eighth grade I was translating for my mother at parent-teacher conferences. I still think in two languages depending on the subject, and I've stopped apologizing for the accent that shows up when I'm tired.
