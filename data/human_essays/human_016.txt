A seventh grader beat me at chess club in eleven moves, and I was furious about it for an embarrassingly long time. I replayed the game in my head for a week trying to find where I went wrong. Eventually I just asked him to show me, which felt humiliating and turned out to be the smartest thing I did all year. He's twelve. I'm sixteen. He still beats me more often than I'd like to admit, but now I ask him to explain it every time, and I've started doing the same for the kids newer than me.
