I broke my arm the second week I tried skateboarding, which felt like a pretty clear sign to stop. My mom certainly thought so. But six weeks in a cast just made me watch more videos and get more stubborn about it, and the day the cast came off I was back at the same curb. I still can't land a kickflip consistently, maybe one in twenty tries, but I can ollie up a curb now without thinking about it, which used to feel impossible. My mom has stopped asking me to stop and started asking me to wear the wrist guards instead.
