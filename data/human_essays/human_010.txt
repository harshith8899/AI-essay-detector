A stress fracture ended my sophomore cross country season three weeks before regionals, and I spent that fall watching from the sideline in a walking boot. It was miserable in a way that's hard to explain to people who don't run. My coach had me keep a training log anyway, just writing down how I felt each day, even doing nothing. When I finally raced again that spring I was slower than I'd ever been. I finished dead last in my first meet back and felt more proud crossing that line than I had at any race before the injury.
