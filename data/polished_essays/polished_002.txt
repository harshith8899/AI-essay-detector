My very first debate round concluded after a mere four minutes when I forgot my entire argument and stood frozen before the judge. It is important to note that the judge visibly set down her pen in disappointment. I wanted desperately to quit that very night, but my partner sent a single, powerful word: 'again.' Consequently, we dedicated ourselves to drilling rebuttals in her basement every Tuesday for an entire month. I continued to lose my next six rounds, yet by the regional tournament I could sense the argument forming even while my opponents were still speaking. Although we did not win the state championship, we advanced to the semifinal round, and I did not freeze once, which felt like a genuine testament to how far I had come.
